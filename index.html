📖 How to Play Double Sage

1. Smart Answer Hints: Every question automatically shows the exact word count, letter count, and digit count (only if digits exist). Example: "Fried Egg" shows 2 words, 8 letters; "459" shows 3 digits.

2. Standard Mode (Setter vs. Guesser): Players take turns setting a secret question and guessing before the timer hits zero. Correct guesses earn +5 pts immediately; missed guesses award +5 pts to the Setter.

3. Fair Contest & Approval: If a Guesser believes their spelling or synonym was judged unfairly, they tap ⚖️ Contest Ruling. The Setter can accept it (transferring the +5 pts to the Guesser) or reject it.

4. Simultaneous Umpire Mode: One Umpire hosts (earning 0 points) and asks 1 to 10 players at once. Everyone who answers right earns +5 pts with their exact speed recorded. If nobody gets it, nobody scores!

5. 📚 My QB's & ✨ AI Suggest: Save your own question banks, unlock Pro QB packs, or tap ✨ Suggest Answer on trivia/math questions (personal questions stay private to you).

📖 How to Play Double Sage

1. Standard Mode (Setter vs. Guesser): Players take turns setting a secret question and answer. You get a smart hint showing word count, letter count, and digits (only when numbers are present). Match the answer before time runs out for +5 pts; miss and the Setter gets +5 pts. Think you were judged unfairly? Tap ⚖️ Contest for Setter review!

2. Simultaneous Umpire Mode (1–10 Players): The Umpire is the organizer (0 points) who sets total rounds and asks all competing players the exact same question at once. Everyone who gets it right earns +5 pts with their speed recorded; if nobody gets it, 0 points are awarded.

3. Smart AI Helper & My QBs: Tap ✨ Suggest when writing questions to auto-solve math (like 20 * 5) or pull factual answers (personal questions like "What is my nickname?" stay private). Save question sets in 📚 My QBs or unlock Pro Packs!
